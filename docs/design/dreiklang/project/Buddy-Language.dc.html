Neu · A guide that lives in the app

Kumpel.

German for “buddy”. A small paper-white face that sits in the corner, explains the page you are on, and walks new learners through the app in one minute. It speaks when spoken to — and it knows when to stay quiet.

Five rules of good company
01
Ask before telling

Opens itself exactly once — the first hello. After that, only you open it.

02
One tip, once

A page earns at most one gentle dot, the first time you visit. Never a popup.

03
Stay out of focus

Hidden while you study, record or sit an exam. Help is a “?” away.

04
Same voice

Plain EN/FR with German words. Short sentences. Never baby-talk, never red.

05
Always dismissible

“Ruhe, bitte” mutes it for good. Settings brings it back.
