A design language for learning German

Colour
is grammar.

The interface is ink and paper. Colour appears only to mean one thing — the gender of a noun — and always arrives with a shape, so it never depends on sight alone.

Four rules the whole product obeys
01
Colour is grammar
Ink and paper everywhere. Blue, rose and yellow are reserved for der, die, das — so a glance is a lesson.
02
German speaks loudest
The target language sets in Jost at display size. Explanations whisper in Inter. The typeface is the language.
03
One thing at a time
One hero and one primary action per screen. Language, theme and speed controls leave the chrome and live where they matter.
04
Feedback you can feel
A right answer clicks shut like a built block. A miss is “Fast!”, never red. Motion, sound and haptics carry the reward.
